AlertBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Welcome to AlertBell. This Privacy Policy explains how AlertBell handles information when you use our mobile application.

AlertBell is a reminder and notification application designed to help users create reminders and receive scheduled alerts, including voice notifications where supported.

Privacy in brief: AlertBell is designed to keep reminder and app settings on your device whenever possible. We do not sell users' personal information.

2. Information We Collect

AlertBell is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Depending on the features you use and the permissions available on your device, AlertBell may process the following information:

  • Reminder titles, messages, dates, and times entered by the user.
  • Reminder repeat settings and notification preferences.
  • App language and theme preferences.
  • Vibration and other local application settings.
  • Notification-related information required to deliver scheduled reminders.
  • Text entered by the user for voice reminder functionality.

The exact information processed may depend on the features and permissions enabled by the user.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

AlertBell may store reminder information and application preferences locally on your device so that the application can provide its core functionality.

Examples may include:

  • Reminder content
  • Reminder date and time
  • Repeat settings
  • Language preference
  • Theme preference
  • Vibration preference
  • Onboarding or application settings

Local information is not intended to be publicly accessible.

4. Notifications

AlertBell uses Android notification functionality to remind you about scheduled reminders.

Notification permission may be required depending on your Android version and device settings.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Voice and Text-to-Speech

AlertBell may use text-to-speech functionality to speak reminder messages.

Text-to-speech processing may be performed by the Android operating system or a compatible text-to-speech service installed on your device.

AlertBell does not use the microphone to record conversations as part of its normal reminder functionality.

6. How We Use Information

Information handled by AlertBell is used only to provide and improve the application's functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing reminders.
  • Scheduling notifications.
  • Providing voice reminder functionality.
  • Remembering application preferences.
  • Maintaining the application's basic functionality.
  • Responding to support requests when you contact us.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

AlertBell is designed so that locally stored reminder information remains on your device unless a feature specifically requires communication with an external service.

If third-party services are added to AlertBell in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is shared, why it is shared, and with whom it is shared.

8. Third-Party Services

AlertBell may rely on Android system services and third-party libraries to provide functionality such as notifications, text-to-speech, application storage, and other technical features.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Developers are responsible for reviewing the data practices of third-party SDKs used in an app and ensuring that the app's Play Console Data Safety information remains accurate.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Reminder information and application preferences stored locally may remain on your device until you delete them, remove them through the application where such functionality is available, clear the application's data, or uninstall AlertBell.

If you contact us by email, we may retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain appropriate support records.

11. Data Deletion

If AlertBell stores information locally on your device, you can remove that information by deleting the relevant reminders, clearing the application's data, or uninstalling the application, subject to the behavior of your Android device and operating system.

If AlertBell introduces an account-based service or server-side personal data in the future, we will provide an appropriate account and data deletion mechanism and update this Privacy Policy.

12. Children's Privacy

AlertBell is not specifically designed for children under the age applicable under local law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality.

13. Permissions

AlertBell may request permissions that are necessary for its functionality, such as notification-related permissions.

Permissions are used only for the purposes described in the application and this Privacy Policy.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to AlertBell, applicable requirements, or our data practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
