AlertBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to AlertBell. This Privacy Policy explains how AlertBell handles information when you use our mobile application.

AlertBell is a reminder and notification application that allows users to create reminders and receive scheduled notifications, including optional voice reminders using text-to-speech functionality supported by the device.

Privacy in brief: AlertBell is designed to keep reminder information and application preferences on your device. AlertBell does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

1. Developer Information

App Name: AlertBell

Package Name: com.sohanstudio.alertbell

Developer: Sohan Studio

Privacy Contact: alertbell.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Process

AlertBell is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The application primarily processes information that users enter or configure for reminder functionality.

Depending on the features you use, AlertBell may process:

  • Reminder titles and messages.
  • Reminder dates and times.
  • Reminder repeat settings.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Language preferences.
  • Theme preferences.
  • Vibration and other application settings.
  • Voice reminder settings.
  • Text used for voice reminder functionality.

AlertBell does not require an account or registration for its core reminder functionality.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

AlertBell stores reminder information and application preferences locally on your device so that the application's core features can continue to function.

Examples of locally stored information include:

  • Reminder titles and messages.
  • Reminder dates and times.
  • Repeat settings.
  • Language preference.
  • Theme preference.
  • Notification preference.
  • Voice reminder settings.
  • Vibration settings.
  • Onboarding and application settings.

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is not intended to be publicly accessible.

4. Notifications

AlertBell uses Android notification functionality to deliver scheduled reminder notifications.

Depending on the Android version and device configuration, notification permission may be required.

Notification permission can be managed by the user through the Android system settings.

AlertBell does not use notifications to collect personal information.

5. Exact Scheduling and Alarms

AlertBell uses Android scheduling capabilities to deliver reminders at the date and time selected by the user.

When precise reminder timing is required, AlertBell may use Android's exact alarm scheduling capabilities, subject to the permissions and restrictions of the Android operating system.

Exact scheduling is used to support the application's reminder functionality and to help deliver reminders as close as possible to the time selected by the user.

Reminder delivery may be affected by Android system settings, battery optimization, notification permissions, Do Not Disturb, battery-saving features, or manufacturer-specific background restrictions.

Users can manage applicable notification and alarm-related settings through Android system settings.

6. Voice and Text-to-Speech

AlertBell may use text-to-speech functionality to speak reminder messages when voice reminders are enabled by the user.

Text-to-speech processing may be performed by the Android operating system or a compatible text-to-speech service installed on the user's device.

AlertBell does not use the microphone to record conversations as part of its normal reminder functionality.

Voice reminder behavior may vary depending on the Android version, device manufacturer, battery optimization settings, background restrictions, device audio settings, and the availability of a compatible text-to-speech service.

7. Background Operation

AlertBell may need to operate through Android's background scheduling and notification mechanisms in order to deliver reminders when the application is not currently visible on the screen.

Background behavior is controlled by the Android operating system and may also be affected by device manufacturer restrictions, battery optimization, power-saving modes, and other system settings.

Because of these system-level restrictions, AlertBell cannot guarantee that voice playback or notification delivery will work identically on every Android device.

8. How We Use Information

Information handled by AlertBell is used only to provide and maintain the application's functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing reminders.
  • Scheduling reminder notifications.
  • Providing voice reminder functionality.
  • Remembering application preferences.
  • Providing language and theme settings.
  • Maintaining basic application functionality.
  • Responding to support requests sent by email.

9. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

AlertBell is designed so that reminder information and application preferences remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to our servers as part of the normal reminder functionality.

If you contact us by email, the information you voluntarily provide in that communication may be received by us so that we can respond to your request.

10. Third-Party Services and Software Libraries

AlertBell may use Android system services and third-party software libraries to provide technical functionality such as notifications, text-to-speech, local storage, and application features.

These services or libraries may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

AlertBell does not intentionally sell or provide users' reminder content to third parties.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by AlertBell.

However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Reminder information and application preferences stored locally may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's data, or uninstall AlertBell.

If you contact us by email, we may retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain appropriate support records.

13. Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored reminder information by deleting reminders within the application, clearing the application's data, or uninstalling AlertBell, subject to the behavior of your Android device and operating system.

Because the core reminder data is stored locally, AlertBell does not currently provide a separate server-side account deletion system.

14. Children's Privacy

AlertBell is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality.

15. Permissions and Device Access

AlertBell requests only the permissions and system capabilities that are necessary to provide its reminder and notification functionality.

  • Notification permission: Used to display scheduled reminder notifications.
  • Exact alarm scheduling: Used when precise reminder scheduling is required for the application's reminder functionality and supported by Android.
  • Background operation: Android may allow or restrict background activity depending on the operating system version, device settings, battery optimization, and manufacturer-specific restrictions.
  • Wake/background-related capabilities: Used where necessary to help Android process scheduled reminder events according to the application's functionality.

AlertBell does not use permissions to intentionally collect personal information unrelated to its reminder functionality.

16. User Control

Users can control their reminders and application preferences from within AlertBell.

Users can also manage notification, alarm, battery, and other applicable system permissions through Android device settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to AlertBell, applicable requirements, or our data practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
