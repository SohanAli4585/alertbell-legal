AlertBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Welcome to AlertBell. This Privacy Policy explains how AlertBell handles information when you use our mobile application.

AlertBell is a reminder and notification application designed to help users create reminders and receive scheduled alerts, including voice reminders through text-to-speech functionality supported by the device.

Privacy in brief: AlertBell is designed to keep reminder information and application settings on your device whenever possible. We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

1. Developer Information

App Name: AlertBell

Package Name: com.example.reminder_with_voice

Developer: Sohan Studio

Privacy Contact: alertbell.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Process

AlertBell is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The application primarily processes information that you enter or configure for reminder functionality.

Depending on the features you use, AlertBell may process:

  • Reminder titles and messages entered by the user.
  • Reminder dates and times.
  • Reminder repeat settings.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Language and theme preferences.
  • Vibration and other application settings.
  • Text used for voice reminder functionality.
  • Information required by the device to schedule and display notifications.

AlertBell does not require an account or registration for its core reminder functionality.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

AlertBell stores reminder information and application preferences locally on your device so that the application's core features can continue to work.

Examples include:

  • Reminder content.
  • Reminder date and time.
  • Repeat settings.
  • Language preference.
  • Theme preference.
  • Vibration preference.
  • Onboarding and application settings.

Locally stored information is not intended to be publicly accessible.

4. Notifications

AlertBell uses Android notification functionality to deliver scheduled reminder notifications.

Depending on the Android version and device configuration, notification permission may be required.

Users can manage notification permissions and related device settings through Android system settings.

5. Exact Scheduling

AlertBell may use Android's scheduling capabilities to provide reminders at the times configured by the user.

The actual delivery of reminders may be affected by Android system settings, battery optimization, device restrictions, notification permissions, Do Not Disturb settings, or manufacturer-specific background activity restrictions.

6. Voice and Text-to-Speech

AlertBell may use text-to-speech functionality to speak reminder messages.

Text-to-speech processing may be performed by the Android operating system or a compatible text-to-speech service installed on your device.

AlertBell does not use the microphone to record conversations as part of its normal reminder functionality.

7. How We Use Information

Information handled by AlertBell is used to provide the application's functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing reminders.
  • Scheduling notifications.
  • Providing voice reminder functionality.
  • Remembering application preferences.
  • Maintaining the application's basic functionality.
  • Providing support when a user contacts us.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

AlertBell is designed so that reminder information and application preferences remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to our servers as part of the normal reminder functionality.

If you contact us by email, the information you provide in that communication may be received by us so that we can respond to your request.

9. Third-Party Services

AlertBell may rely on Android system services and third-party software libraries to provide features such as notifications, text-to-speech, local storage, and other technical functionality.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

AlertBell does not intentionally sell or provide users' reminder information to third parties.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by AlertBell.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Reminder information and application preferences stored locally may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's data, or uninstall AlertBell.

If you contact us by email, we may retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain appropriate support records.

12. Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored reminder information by deleting reminders within the application, clearing the application's data, or uninstalling AlertBell, subject to the behavior of your Android device and operating system.

Because the core reminder data is stored locally, AlertBell does not currently provide a separate server-side account deletion system.

13. Children's Privacy

AlertBell is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality.

14. Permissions

AlertBell may request permissions or system access that are necessary for its functionality, such as notification and scheduling-related capabilities.

These permissions are used to provide the features described in this Privacy Policy and within the application.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to AlertBell, applicable requirements, or our data practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
